# Python Barcelona, since 2007

The Python community of Barcelona: a [meetup group](https://www.meetup.com/python-barcelona/) since 2007, a [non-profit association](https://pybcn.org/pybcn_association/information/) since 2018, and an [archive](https://pybcn.org/) of everything in between.

We run the [monthly PyBCN meetups](https://www.meetup.com/python-barcelona/), the [PyLadies BCN workshops](https://www.meetup.com/pyladies-bcn/), the practice sessions and coding dojos, and the annual [PyDay BCN](https://pybcn.org/events/pyday_bcn/). The events are free or low cost, held in Barcelona, and hosted by local companies and universities.

## Take part

- [Become a member](https://pybcn.org/pybcn_association/membership/): 20 euros a year, and a voice and a vote in the general assembly.
- [Organize with us](https://pybcn.org/pybcn_association/organize-with-us/): help us run a meetup, or ask us to help with yours.
- [Propose a talk](https://pybcn.org/pybcn_association/propose-a-talk/): twenty minutes on something you built, learnt, or broke.
- [Sponsor us](https://pybcn.org/pybcn_association/sponsor-us/), or [contact us](https://pybcn.org/contact/) with a question, an idea, or a venue to offer.

A non-profit run by volunteers: these are [the people behind it](https://pybcn.org/pybcn_association/organizers/). Every event follows our [Code of Conduct](https://pybcn.org/pybcn_association/coc/).
